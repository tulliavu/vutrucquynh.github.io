Vu Truc Quynh

I build optimization models for electric vehicle charging infrastructure. Master of Philosophy candidate at the Graduate School of Innovation and Practice for Smart Society (SmaSo), Hiroshima University, supervised by Prof. Ayyoob Sharifi and Assistant Professor Omar Sarif.

Siting and sizing a public charging network is a facility location problem: fixed costs, capacity limits and demand that shifts with every station you add. I formulate it as a multi-objective optimization problem and solve it in Python, with Ho Chi Minh City as the case study.

I came to this from international economics. What holds my attention is the optimization: stating an infrastructure problem precisely enough that a solver can answer it. I am looking to collaborate on optimization and programming applications in EV management, so please write to me.

Siting Optimization of Public Electric Vehicle Charging Stations: A Case Study of Ho Chi Minh City

Master’s thesis · Supervised by Prof. Ayyoob Sharifi and Assistant Professor Omar Sarif
Advisory: Dr. Nguyen Bao Huy, School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology

Where stations are placed, and how much capacity each one gets, decides whether the network is usable at all. The thesis treats this as a constrained optimization problem over Ho Chi Minh City rather than a planning judgement.
