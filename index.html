Vu Truc Quynh

I build optimization models for electric vehicle charging infrastructure. Master of Philosophy candidate at the Graduate School of Innovation and Practice for Smart Society (SmaSo), Hiroshima University — supervised by Prof. Ayyoob Sharifi and Assistant Professor Omar.

Siting and sizing a public charging network is a facility location problem: fixed costs, capacity limits and demand that shifts with every station you add. I formulate it as a mixed integer nonlinear program and solve it in Python, with Ho Chi Minh City as the case study.

I came to this from international economics, and what holds my attention is the optimization — stating an infrastructure problem precisely enough that a solver can answer it. I am looking to collaborate on optimization and programming applications in EV management — write to me.

Siting Optimization of Public Electric Vehicle Charging Stations: A Case Study of Ho Chi Minh City

Master’s thesis · Supervised by Prof. Ayyoob Sharifi and Assistant Professor Omar · Advisory: Dr. Huy

Where stations are placed, and how much capacity each one gets, decides whether the network is usable at all. The thesis treats this as a constrained optimization problem over Ho Chi Minh City rather than a planning judgement.

The mixed integer nonlinear formulation of the same problem, and the starting point for the current work.
