Vu Truc Quynh is a Master's student at Hiroshima University, supervised by Professor Ayyoob Sharifi and Professor Omar. Her research focuses on optimal siting of public electric vehicle charging stations (EVCS) in Ho Chi Minh City, combining GIS analysis, spatial modeling, and infrastructure planning methodologies to support Vietnam's transition to sustainable urban mobility.

Research Focus

Master's Thesis: EVCS Siting Optimization in Ho Chi Minh City

Supervised by Prof. Ayyoob Sharifi & Prof. Omar · Advisory: Dr. Huy

This research addresses the critical challenge of strategic placement of public electric vehicle charging stations to maximize coverage and accessibility across Ho Chi Minh City. The study integrates multiple methodologies including:

  • Geographic Information Systems (GIS) analysis
  • Spatial optimization modeling
  • Accessibility and demand assessment
  • Infrastructure sustainability considerations
