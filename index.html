Research

Siting optimisation for public EV charging stations in Ho Chi Minh City

Master’s thesis · Supervised by Prof. Ayyoob Sharifi and Prof. Omar · Advisory: Dr. Huy

Where charging stations are placed decides who can actually use them. This thesis looks for placements that maximise coverage and accessibility across the city, drawing on:

  • Geographic Information Systems (GIS) analysis
  • Spatial optimisation modelling
  • Accessibility and demand assessment
  • Infrastructure sustainability considerations
